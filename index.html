Introduction

"What you cannot see cannot hurt you, but you never know if what scares you is simply hiding in plain sight"

Hello, my name is Harvey, a current junior at Metro Early College High School. I participate in a couple clubs, FRC Robotics, E-Sports, and Yearbook, to be exact. However, in the past few weeks there has been a something peculiar that has caught my eye. Those who lurk in the shadows have challenged all I once believed. They've torn down walls of trust I've built, making me to doubt those I once loved. Those wicked creatures that should've never seen the light of day during their eternal rest. One above all whose wickedness surpasses that of ruthless tyrants. The very the reason I keep one eye open through the broadest of nights.
